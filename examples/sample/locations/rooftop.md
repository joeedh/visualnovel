A flat concrete rooftop ringed by a chain-link fence, the city rooftops and a smudge of distant hills beyond. A squat green water tank stands in one corner; a few forgotten desks have been pushed against the stairwell housing. In the evening the low sun rakes long shadows across the concrete and gilds the fence wire.
