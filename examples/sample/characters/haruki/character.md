Haruki is a seventeen-year-old student with close-cropped black hair that falls in loose strands across his forehead, and striking orange eyes that catch the light like amber. He wears the school's black gakuran uniform — a high-collared jacket with brass buttons fastened to the throat over straight black trousers — pressed but worn comfortably, sleeves a touch long.
