EXT. ROOFTOP - EVENING

Aiko pushes through the heavy door to find the rooftop already occupied. Haruki leans against the fence, hands in his pockets, watching the courtyard lights flicker on.

AIKO
Oh — sorry. I didn't think anyone came up here.

HARUKI
Most people don't. That's the appeal.

He glances at her, then back to the view. Aiko hesitates, then steps up beside him.

AIKO
The new girl gets the quiet places, I guess.

HARUKI
Stick around. They stop being quiet.

A faint, dry smile. Aiko finds herself smiling too.
